# The five colors (color pie)

Each color has a **philosophy** and typical **mechanics**. Decks are built in one or more colors.

## At a glance

| Color | Land | Philosophy (short) | Often good at |
|-------|------|-------------------|---------------|
| **White (W)** | Plains | Order, community, rules | Small creatures, protection, board wipes, life gain |
| **Blue (U)** | Island | Knowledge, logic, control | Counterspells, card draw, bounce, stealing time |
| **Black (B)** | Swamp | Ambition, power at any cost | Destroy creatures, discard, reanimation, pay life |
| **Red (R)** | Mountain | Freedom, emotion, speed | Direct damage (burn), haste, aggressive creatures |
| **Green (G)** | Forest | Nature, growth, big creatures | Large creatures, mana ramp, fight spells, artifact/enchantment destruction |

## Color wheel relationships

```
        White
       /     \
   Green       Blue
      |         |
    Red ---- Black
```

- **Allies** (next on wheel): W–U, U–B, B–R, R–G, G–W  
- **Enemies** (opposite): W–B, U–R, etc.

## Tutorial / starter context

- **White tutorial** (e.g. Kylea): Plains, small white creatures, lifelink/vigilance themes.
- **Color Challenges** in Arena teach one color at a time with a mono-colored deck.

## Agent notes (play style by color)

| Color | Aggro vs control | Typical win |
|-------|------------------|-------------|
| White | Midrange / control | Many small creatures + removal |
| Blue | Control | Outvalue, counter key spells, win late |
| Black | Midrange / control | Removal + recurring threats |
| Red | Aggro | Burn + fast creatures |
| Green | Aggro / midrange | Big creatures + combat |
