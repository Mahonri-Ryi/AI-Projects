# Mana and lands

## Five colors of mana

| Color | Symbol | Basic land | Mana produced |
|-------|--------|------------|---------------|
| **White** | W | **Plains** | White mana |
| **Blue** | U | **Island** | Blue mana |
| **Black** | B | **Swamp** | Black mana |
| **Red** | R | **Mountain** | Red mana |
| **Green** | G | **Forest** | Green mana |

Order on the back of cards (color wheel): **W → U → B → R → G** (clockwise). Adjacent colors are **allies**; opposite colors are **enemies**.

## Basic lands

- Only five **basic** land names: Plains, Island, Swamp, Mountain, Forest.
- Each basic land has an implicit ability: tap → add one mana of that color.
- Other lands (e.g. “Temple”, “Dual Land”) are **nonbasic** and have special rules text.

## Playing lands

- **When**: Your main phase, stack empty, one per turn.
- **How (Arena)**: **Drag** from hand to an empty spot on **your** side of the battlefield.
- **How (paper)**: Play face up onto the battlefield (no casting cost).
- Lands enter **untapped** unless an effect says otherwise.

## Using mana from lands

- **Tap** the land to add mana (in Arena, lands are often auto-tapped when you cast a spell).
- Tapped lands untap in your **untap step** at the start of your turn.
- You need the **right colors** for colored costs in spell mana costs.

## Generic mana

- A number in a grey circle (e.g. `{2}`) = **generic** mana — any color or colorless can pay it.
- Example: `{1}{W}` = one mana of any type **plus** one white mana.

## Mana pool

- Mana you’ve produced but not spent sits in your **mana pool** briefly.
- Arena auto-assigns and clears mana; don’t worry about manual pool management as a beginner.

## Deck building hint

- Most decks use **24–26 lands** in a 60-card deck (varies by strategy).
- Mono-color tutorial decks use only one basic land type (e.g. white tutorial → **Plains**).

## Common beginner mistakes

| Mistake | Correct rule |
|---------|----------------|
| Playing two lands one turn (without effects) | One land drop per turn |
| “Casting” a land like a spell | Play land from hand to battlefield |
| Click-click instead of drag in Arena | Drag hand → battlefield |
| Forgetting to tap lands for expensive spells | Need enough **sources** of each color |
